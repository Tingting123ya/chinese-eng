CONTRASTIVE PHONETIC GUIDE (Zhuyin ⇄ Pinyin)
Master Chinese Sounds with English References
A

Learn why Pinyin sounds differ from standard English phonics and how Zhuyin (Bopomofo) prevents confusing unaspirated and aspirated consonants.

1st Tone (High Flat) ˉ
mā (ㄇㄚ)
High, flat pitch. Like holding a steady musical note "Do".
2nd Tone (Rising) ˊ
má (ㄇㄚˊ)
Rising pitch, exactly like asking a surprised question: "What?!".
3rd Tone (Dipping) ˇ
mǎ (ㄇㄚˇ)
Dips low then rises slightly: like a hesitant "Weeeell...".
4th Tone (Falling) ˋ
mà (ㄇㄚˋ)
Sharp, decisive drop: like giving a firm, authoritative command: "NO!".
